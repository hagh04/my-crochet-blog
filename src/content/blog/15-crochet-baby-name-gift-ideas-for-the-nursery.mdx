A baby's name is one of the sweetest details to incorporate into nursery décor, and crochet gives you plenty of creative ways to display it. You can stitch a name directly onto a blanket, create individual crochet letters for a wall hanging, or turn an alphabet pattern into a colorful bunting banner.

For this collection, I focused on **free crochet patterns and tutorials** that can genuinely be used to create personalized baby-name gifts. I also avoided relying on the same Moogly alphabet entry repeatedly, since there are several other approaches available.

# Final Thoughts

Personalized crochet nursery gifts can be as simple or elaborate as you want. A **single initial wall hanging** is perfect for a minimalist nursery, while a full **crochet name banner** makes a more playful statement. If you want something useful as well as decorative, adding a name to a baby blanket creates a keepsake that can stay with the child long after the nursery years.

For the most flexible projects, **crochet alphabet appliqués, C2C letters, and alphabet bunting** give you the freedom to spell almost any name and choose your own colors. The key is to keep the lettering and palette consistent with the nursery so the handmade name piece feels like part of the room rather than an unrelated decoration.

**Note:** I deliberately did **not** include the paid Personalized Baby Name Blanket by Heidi Filbert or paid alphabet/name patterns from Etsy/Ravelry as free-pattern entries. The Heidi Filbert pattern is currently listed at $4.99, while the Silke Merckx alphabet pattern is listed at $4.00.
