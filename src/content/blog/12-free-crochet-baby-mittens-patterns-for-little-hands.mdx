## Introduction

Tiny handmade mittens are among the sweetest projects to add to a crochet baby collection. They're quick to make, use relatively little yarn, and can be customized with soft colors, pretty cuffs, and delicate textures. Baby mittens can also help keep little hands warm and reduce accidental scratching from sharp fingernails. In this roundup, you'll find 12 free crochet baby mitten patterns with a variety of styles and skill levels, including simple newborn mitts, cozy winter designs, and decorative patterns that make lovely handmade gifts. Whether you're preparing a baby shower present or completing a matching layette, there's a charming project here to inspire your next make.

## Final Thoughts

These 12 free crochet baby mitten patterns offer plenty of inspiration, whether you're looking for a simple newborn design, a textured cuff, a plush winter accessory, or a playful animal-inspired pair. If you're a beginner, start with a no-thumb mitten made using basic stitches. If you want something more decorative, choose a pattern with ribbing, bobbles, or a textured stitch.

Baby mittens are also wonderful stash-busting projects, since most pairs require relatively little yarn. Choose a soft, washable fiber, check the pattern's sizing carefully, and consider coordinating the mittens with a baby hat, cardigan, or blanket to create a thoughtful handmade gift.

A quick safety reminder: Baby mittens should fit comfortably and be used under appropriate supervision. Avoid loose ties, detachable embellishments, and small parts that could become hazards. For premature babies or hospital donations, always follow the receiving organization's specific requirements.
