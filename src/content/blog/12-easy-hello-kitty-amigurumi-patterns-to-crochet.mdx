## Introduction

Hello Kitty makes such a fun crochet project, especially if you love creating cute little stuffed characters! Her simple shape, pointed ears, tiny nose, whiskers, and signature bow make her a wonderful choice for practicing amigurumi techniques. In this roundup, you'll find 12 easy Hello Kitty amigurumi patterns to crochet, including miniature dolls, classic plushies, and character-inspired designs. Some are ideal for confident beginners, while others are great next steps once you're comfortable crocheting in the round. Grab your favorite yarn, a crochet hook, and some stuffing, and let's find your next adorable project!

A quick note before you begin: I've prioritized free patterns and free tutorials. Skill levels are approximate when a designer doesn't specify one, so check the original instructions before starting.

## Final Thoughts

These 12 easy Hello Kitty amigurumi patterns to crochet offer a variety of projects, from tiny collectibles and simple dolls to themed characters and outfit variations. If you're just starting out, choose a design with a photo tutorial or video instructions. Once you're comfortable with single crochet, increases, decreases, and sewing pieces together, you can try more detailed designs.

Before you start, gather your yarn, hook, stuffing, stitch markers, and yarn needle. If the toy is intended for a young child, embroidered facial details are generally safer than detachable plastic pieces. Also, check the designer's usage terms before sharing or selling finished projects.

Happy crocheting!
