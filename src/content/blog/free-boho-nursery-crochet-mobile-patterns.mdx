A crochet nursery mobile is such a sweet way to bring handmade charm into a baby's room. Instead of choosing a mass-produced decoration, you can crochet something with your own color palette, textures, and personality. Boho nursery decor works especially beautifully with soft creams, dusty pinks, sage greens, mustard, terracotta, muted blue, and natural wood. I rounded up six free crochet mobile patterns that can be adapted beautifully to this relaxed style, whether you're decorating your own nursery or making a memorable baby-shower gift.

> **Safety note:** Crochet mobiles are decorative items and should always be securely assembled and positioned completely out of a baby's reach. Follow the original designer's assembly instructions carefully and regularly check knots, attachments, beads, and other small components.

## 1. Moon Bunny and Stars Crochet Mobile

The **Moon Bunny and Stars Crochet Mobile** from Octopus Crochet is an especially charming choice for a dreamy boho nursery. The design combines a large crescent-style moon, a tiny bunny, stars, and small hanging elements to create a whimsical night-sky scene. The designer explains that the moon is worked with thicker yarn while the bunny and stars use thinner yarn, giving the finished mobile an attractive variation in scale. The free version is available directly on the designer's blog, while an ad-free PDF is also offered.

This free design features a crocheted rainbow with clouds and metallic details, creating a cheerful hanging decoration that can easily be adapted for nursery use. The pattern uses basic stitches for most of the motifs, while the clouds provide a slightly more challenging element. It is available as a free Ravelry download in both US and UK terminology.

## Final Thoughts

A handmade crochet mobile can become one of the sweetest decorative details in a nursery. Whether you prefer the dreamy moon-and-bunny theme, a muted rainbow, cheerful clouds, an ocean-inspired scene, whimsical hot-air balloons, or playful dinosaurs, these free patterns give you plenty of room to personalize the colors and materials.

For a true **boho nursery crochet mobile**, natural wooden hoops, muted earthy colors, soft cotton yarn, simple shapes, and warm neutrals are an easy combination. Most importantly, choose a design that reflects the nursery's personality and take your time with the finishing and assembly.

If you find a pattern you love, visit the **original designer's website** through the links above and follow their complete instructions. Supporting the original designers by visiting their pages helps keep free crochet patterns available to the crafting community.
