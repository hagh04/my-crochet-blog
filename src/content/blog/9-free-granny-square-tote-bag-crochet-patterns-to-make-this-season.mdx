## Introduction

There’s a reason granny square bags continue to be so popular: they’re colorful, customizable, wonderfully nostalgic, and surprisingly practical. A simple collection of squares can become a roomy everyday tote, a sturdy shopping bag, or a beautiful statement accessory simply by changing the colors, yarn, handles, or joining method. For this directory, I searched for free patterns from original designer and publisher pages and selected a variety of granny square tote styles, from beginner-friendly projects to more involved designs. Grab your favorite yarn and get ready to find your next bag!

## 2. Granny Square Tote by Delia Creates

**Original Pattern:** [Delia Creates](https://www.deliacreates.com/granny-square-tote-free-crochet-pattern/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

The **Granny Square Tote by Delia Creates** takes oversized granny squares and turns them into an impressively sturdy everyday bag. What makes this design especially interesting is that each square is worked using four strands of worsted-weight yarn together, creating a dense fabric with considerably less stretch than a traditional single-strand granny square. The finished tote is approximately 15.5 inches tall and 15 inches wide, with handles adding additional height. A video tutorial is also included to help crocheters through the construction.

* **Original Source:** Delia Creates

Delia recommends inexpensive acrylic yarn for the main color because it helps create a sturdy, less-stretchy bag. Choose a neutral main color and let colorful squares become the focal point, or go bold with several contrasting shades for a cheerful retro look.

## Final Thoughts

Granny square tote bags are wonderfully versatile because there really is no single “right” way to make one. You can use tiny colorful squares, oversized motifs, floral centers, neutral borders, chunky yarn, cotton DK, or even multiple strands of worsted yarn to completely change the character of your finished bag.

If you’re new to crochet bags, start with a simpler beginner-friendly design such as the **Totes Crochet Bag** or **Big Granny Tote**. If you’re ready for more construction and color planning, the **Bella Coco**, **Annie Design Crochet**, or **Delia Creates** designs are excellent choices.

And perhaps the best part? **You don’t have to copy the sample colors.** Lay out your favorite yarn shades, experiment with different combinations, and make a granny square tote that feels completely yours.
