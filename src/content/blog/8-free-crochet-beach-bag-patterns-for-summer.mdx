## Introduction

Summer is the perfect excuse to pick up your crochet hook and create something beautiful that you can actually use! A handmade beach bag is one of those satisfying projects that combines creativity with practicality. Whether you need a roomy tote for towels and sunscreen or a lightweight mesh bag for your summer essentials, there are so many wonderful designs to explore.

For this collection, I’ve gathered eight free crochet beach bag patterns with different shapes, stitch textures, and construction techniques. You’ll find beginner-friendly projects, stylish striped designs, textured shell stitches, and natural-looking raffia bags. Each pattern brings something different to the table, so you can choose the one that best matches your crochet experience and personal style.

Let's discover your next summer crochet project!

---

## 1. Free Crochet Beach Bag Pattern – HanJan Crochet

**Original free pattern:** [HanJan Crochet](https://www.hanjancrochet.com/free-crochet-beach-bag-pattern/)

If you’re dreaming of a generously sized beach tote that can carry everything you need for a full day outdoors, this design is a wonderful place to start. Hannah Cross created a modern crochet beach bag featuring an attractive mesh pattern and practical integrated straps. One of the most appealing details is its construction from seven simple rectangles, which makes the project approachable without sacrificing style. The finished bag has plenty of room for your towel, sunglasses, sunscreen, water bottle, and even a small crochet project for relaxing by the sea.

### Pattern Details

* **Skill Level:** Beginner
* **Designer:** Hannah Cross
* **Yarn:** Worsted-weight cotton
* **Hook:** 4.5 mm
* **Construction:** Seven crocheted rectangles
* **Finished Size:** Approximately 19.5 inches wide × 11.5 inches tall, excluding straps
* **Special Features:** Mesh texture, integrated straps, adjustable height and handle length
* **Tutorial:** Step-by-step video tutorial available

For a classic coastal look, try combining ecru with denim blue, just as the designer does. You could also create a soft neutral version with sand and ivory or a cheerful summer combination with coral and turquoise. Cotton yarn is an excellent choice because it creates a durable fabric suitable for regular use. This tote would make a lovely handmade gift for someone who enjoys beach outings, picnics, or weekend market trips.

**Ready to get started? Visit HanJan Crochet for the free pattern and video tutorial.**

## 3. Salt + Sand Beach Bag – MJ’s off the Hook Designs

**Original free pattern:** [MJ’s off the Hook Designs](https://www.mjsoffthehookdesigns.com/free-crochet-beach-bag-pattern-salt-sand-beach-bag/)

If you love crochet accessories with beautiful stitch definition, the Salt + Sand Beach Bag deserves a place on your summer project list. Designed by Michelle Moore, this bag features a lovely shell-stitch texture that creates a decorative, almost wave-like appearance. It is worked seamlessly from the bottom up and includes a drawstring-style opening with a removable lace tie. That extra detail makes the design particularly versatile: the tie can also be worn as a coordinating headband or hair accessory.

* **Designer:** Michelle Moore
* **Yarn:** Worsted-weight cotton for the bag, with DK-weight cotton for the lace tie
* **Hooks:** 6 mm and 4 mm
* **Finished Size:** Approximately 14 × 16.5 inches

* **Special Features:** Shell stitch, drawstring opening, removable lace tie

This bag would look beautiful in cream, sage green, or soft ocean blue. Cotton yarn helps the stitches stand out while creating a practical accessory for beach trips and everyday errands. ## 6. Raffia Crochet Beach Bag – Nourish and Nestle

**Original free pattern:** [Nourish and Nestle](https://nourishandnestle.com/free-crochet-beach-bag-pattern-made-with-raffia-yarn/)

For a beach bag with a natural, boutique-inspired appearance, this raffia design is a beautiful choice. Rather than using traditional cotton yarn, the bag is crocheted with raffia, which gives it a firmer structure and a lovely summery finish. The construction begins with a circular base before continuing upward into the body and handles. Its relatively simple stitch pattern lets the raffia take center stage, creating an accessory that feels equally suitable for beach days, farmers’ markets, and weekend holidays.

<CloudImage src="https://res.cloudinary.com/cjqufjgq/image/upload/v1791661235/Crochet-Beach-Bag-34-of-43-683x1024.jpg" alt="## Raffia Crochet Beach Bag – Nourish and Nestle" />

### Pattern Details

* **Skill Level:** Easy
* **Designer:** Nourish and Nestle
* **Yarn:** Raffia yarn, approximately 500 yards
* **Hook:** 4 mm
* **Finished Size:** Approximately 14 inches wide
* **Construction:** Worked in joined rounds, beginning with a circular base
* **Special Features:** Natural texture, structured handles, minimal shaping
* **Finishing:** Blocking recommended for a neat shape

Natural raffia creates a timeless neutral bag that pairs beautifully with linen dresses, sandals, and other warm-weather clothing. If you prefer a little color, look for raffia in different shades or add a contrasting scarf or decorative tie to the handle. The firm texture helps the finished bag maintain its shape, making it a great option for anyone who prefers a more structured accessory. It would also make a lovely handmade gift for someone who enjoys natural fibers and understated summer style.

**Visit Nourish and Nestle for the free raffia crochet beach bag pattern and helpful finishing tips.**

## Final Thoughts

A handmade crochet beach bag is a wonderful summer project because it combines creativity, practicality, and personal style. Whether you prefer the nautical stripes of the **Fosbas Designs Coastal Stripes Beach Bag**, the elegant shell stitches of the **Salt + Sand Beach Bag**, or the natural look of the **raffia tote from Nourish and Nestle**, there is a design here to suit different tastes and crochet experience levels.

If you’re a beginner, consider starting with the **Lovable Loops Beach Bag** or the **HanJan Crochet design**. If you’re ready to try something more decorative, the **Day at the Beach Bag** and **Seashore Tote** offer interesting stitch patterns and finishing details.

Whichever design you choose, remember that the best beach bag is one you’ll enjoy making and carrying. Experiment with your favorite colors, choose yarn that suits your intended use, and have fun creating an accessory that feels uniquely yours.

Happy crocheting, and enjoy your summer stitching!
