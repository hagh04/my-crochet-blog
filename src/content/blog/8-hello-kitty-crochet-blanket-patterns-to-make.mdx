## Introduction

If you love Hello Kitty and crochet, combining the two into a cozy blanket is such a fun way to celebrate this beloved character! From oversized kitty faces surrounded by bright colors to charming granny squares that you can join one at a time, there are several ways to create a blanket that feels personal and special. In this roundup, I've gathered eight Hello Kitty-inspired crochet blanket ideas, including free graphs, character squares, and blanket-building motifs. Some projects are best for confident beginners, while others are more suitable if you already enjoy colorwork and graphgan crochet.

A helpful tip: Not every resource below is a complete blanket pattern. Some provide a full graph or written instructions, while others offer individual squares or motifs that you can join to create a blanket of your preferred size. Check each original designer's page for the exact instructions and current availability.

## Final Thoughts

These eight Hello Kitty crochet blanket ideas offer several ways to turn a favorite character into a cozy handmade project. If you enjoy large, colorful designs, try a C2C graphghan. If you prefer smaller projects that you can work on whenever you have a spare moment, granny squares and hexagon motifs are excellent alternatives.

Here are my recommendations for choosing your first project:

* For graphgan fans: Start with Magic Yarn Pixels or Kendra's large graph.
* For modular blankets: Try the granny squares from GrannySquare.Me or Secret of Grandma.
* For smaller projects: Explore The Crochet Nook's compact graph or the mini-blanket idea.
* For a unique layout: Experiment with kitty-inspired hexagons.

Before starting, check the original designer's page for the complete instructions, yarn requirements, and current availability. Some resources are motifs or graphs rather than complete blanket patterns, so you may need to plan your own joining method and final dimensions.

Most importantly, have fun choosing your colors and creating a blanket that reflects your own style. A handmade Hello Kitty-inspired blanket can become a treasured gift, a cheerful bedroom accessory, or a cozy addition to your crochet collection.

Happy crocheting!
