## Introduction

There’s something wonderfully nostalgic about a granny square bag, but these crochet designs prove that this classic motif can look just as fresh and modern today. Granny squares are perfect for experimenting with color, using up yarn scraps, and crocheting your project in small, portable pieces before assembling everything into a beautiful finished bag. Whether you’re searching for a practical tote, a compact crossbody, or a colorful everyday purse, this collection of free granny square bag patterns has plenty of inspiration for your next crochet project.

## 1. Halcyon Granny Square Bag

**Original Pattern:** [Make & Do Crew](https://makeanddocrew.com/granny-square-bag-crochet-pattern/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

The **Halcyon Granny Square Bag** gives the traditional granny square a modern sling-bag makeover. Designed by Jess Coppom of Make & Do Crew, this portable project uses 11 granny squares that are joined together to create a compact bag with an adjustable strap. The construction is particularly interesting because the squares are arranged and folded to form the bag shape rather than simply being joined into a basic rectangle. The free pattern also includes a step-by-step tutorial, making the construction easier to follow.

<CloudImage src="https://res.cloudinary.com/cjqufjgq/image/upload/v1790956281/bag.jpg" alt="## Halcyon Granny Square Bag" />

### Pattern Details

* **Skill Level:** Beginner
* **Designer:** Jess Coppom / Make & Do Crew
* **Original Source:** Make & Do Crew
* **Key Features:** 11 granny squares, sling-bag construction, adjustable strap, join-as-you-go techniques, optional button and leather strap

For the original look, the designer uses Lion Brand Pima Cotton in a muted combination of shades. Cotton is particularly useful for crochet bags because it is sturdy and washable. You can also personalize the design with scrap yarn, a single-color palette, a wooden button, or a leather strap. It’s a lovely choice for everyday outfits, vacations, and anyone who enjoys portable crochet projects that can be worked square by square.

**Ready to make this stylish sling bag?** Visit [Make & Do Crew](https://makeanddocrew.com/granny-square-bag-crochet-pattern/?utm_source=chatgpt.com) for the free Halcyon Granny Square Bag pattern and tutorial.

**Original Pattern:** [Jewels and Jones](https://jewelsandjones.com/crochet-granny-square-bag-free-crochet-pattern/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

If you love the look of granny squares but prefer a clean and understated color palette, the **Crochet Granny Square Bag from Jewels and Jones** is a beautiful option. * **Designer:** Nicky / Jewels and Jones
* **Original Source:** Jewels and Jones
* **Key Features:** 13 moss-stitch squares, sturdy tote shape, simple stitches, crocheted handles, adjustable strap length

The original sample uses a soft parchment shade with charcoal seams, but this design would look equally lovely in cream and black, earthy neutrals, or a colorful combination of cotton yarns. The designer recommends worsted-weight yarn and notes several cotton alternatives.
