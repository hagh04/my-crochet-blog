There is nothing quite like the joy of welcoming a new little one into the world, and what better way to show your love than with a handmade gift? Crocheting for babies is incredibly rewarding because the projects are relatively quick to finish, use minimal yarn, and are destined to be treasured as family keepsakes. Whether you are prepping for an upcoming baby shower, making a special heirloom for a grandchild, or simply looking to expand your stitch horizons, I’ve rounded up some of the sweetest free crochet patterns available online. From cozy blankets and tiny garments to playful nursery toys, these hand-picked patterns are sure to inspire your next creative project. Grab your favorite hook, settle in, and let's explore these lovely designs!

---

### 2. Avery Baby Blanket

If you are looking for a relaxing, rhythmic project that produces a beautiful texture, the Avery Baby Blanket is a wonderful choice. Designed with a simple one-row repeat, this blanket lets you easily unwind while creating a wonderfully thick and comforting fabric. Its modern look is characterized by subtle horizontal ridges that add depth and visual interest without feeling stiff or heavy. This blanket is ideal for wrapping up a newborn during stroller walks, and its simple construction means you will enjoy every single stitch from start to finish.

<CloudImage src="https://res.cloudinary.com/dbjxr1kdv/image/upload/v1783175315/Easy-striped-crochet-baby-blanke_pmsbqx.webp" alt="### Avery Baby Blanket" />

#### Pattern Details

* **Skill Level:** Beginner
* **Designer:** Julie Bonner
* **Original Source:** [I Can Crochet That](https://icancrochetthat.com/)

The Avery blanket really shines when worked with self-striping cake yarns, allowing the colors to transition seamlessly without any extra end-weaving on your part. A soft wool-blend or a lightweight premium acrylic is ideal for providing year-round warmth. It makes a highly practical, comforting baby shower gift that parents can easily machine wash and tumble dry without worry.

**Where to find the pattern:** Head over to [**I Can Crochet That**](https://icancrochetthat.com/avery-crochet-baby-blanket-pattern/) to access this free blanket pattern.
