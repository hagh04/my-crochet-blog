# Final Thoughts

These **free Hello Kitty crochet patterns** show just how many different ways you can bring the beloved cat-inspired character into your crochet projects. If you're looking for a classic stuffed figure, the patterns from **Snacksies Handicraft, Ellie Loh, Lovelycraft, Sweetsamdesign, and Ribblr** give you several amigurumi options. For something smaller and quicker, the keychain patterns are ideal for using small amounts of yarn and creating handmade gifts.

You can also take Hello Kitty beyond amigurumi with a **purse, pouch, or beanie**. When choosing yarn, smooth cotton or acrylic is useful when you want crisp facial details, while plush yarn can create a softer stuffed character. Just remember to check each designer's current usage terms before selling finished items or using a pattern commercially, as permissions differ between designers.
