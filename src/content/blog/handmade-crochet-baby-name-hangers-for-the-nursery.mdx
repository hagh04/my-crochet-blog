A handmade **crochet baby name hanger** is a beautiful way to personalize a nursery without relying on mass-produced décor. Whether you spell the baby's full name with individual crochet letters, create a single monogram, or combine letters with clouds, stars, flowers, and rainbows, there are plenty of ways to make the finished piece feel unique. The free patterns below give you different approaches to creating personalized nursery décor, from flat appliqué letters to C2C designs and three-dimensional crochet lettering.

> **Safety note:** These projects are intended as wall décor. They should be securely mounted and kept completely out of a baby's reach.

# Final Thoughts

These **12 crochet baby name hanger ideas** show how versatile free crochet alphabet patterns can be. Rather than searching for a single pattern that already spells a specific baby's name, you can choose a free alphabet collection and build a completely personalized piece around it.

For a **simple project**, flat appliqué letters from Moogly are probably the easiest choice. For something more substantial, the **C2C Alphabet Wall Hanging** gives you a ready-made wall-hanging format, while the **3D letters from The Crochet Crowd** create a more dimensional nursery decoration.

For colors, cream, oatmeal, beige, sage, dusty rose, muted blue, and warm brown all work beautifully for nursery décor. A wooden dowel, natural branch, or simple hoop can finish the hanger while keeping attention on the baby's name.
