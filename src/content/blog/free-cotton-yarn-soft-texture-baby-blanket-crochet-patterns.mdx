Nothing compares to the comfort of a handmade baby blanket crafted with soft cotton yarn. Cotton is breathable, gentle against delicate skin, and creates beautiful stitch definition that highlights every textured detail. Whether you're making a thoughtful baby shower gift or creating a treasured keepsake for your own little one, these free crochet blanket patterns combine cozy texture with timeless style. From simple beginner-friendly stitches to elegant heirloom-inspired designs, you'll find plenty of inspiration for your next project.
