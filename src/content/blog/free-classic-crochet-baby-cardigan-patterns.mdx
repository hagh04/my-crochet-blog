A handmade baby cardigan is one of those crochet projects that never really goes out of style. Whether it has a simple button front, a pretty shell stitch, delicate ruffles, or a traditional raglan shape, a crocheted cardigan can become a favorite little layer for everyday wear and special occasions. We’ve gathered **15 classic crochet baby cardigan patterns** that offer a beautiful mix of timeless designs, vintage-inspired details, beginner-friendly construction, and practical sizing. From tiny newborn cardigans to designs that grow with baby, there is something here for every crocheter.

---

## 1. Classic Crochet Baby Cardigan

The **Classic Crochet Baby Cardigan by Marianna Mel** is a wonderful example of a timeless baby garment. Designed for an average 9–12-month-old baby, the cardigan is worked in one piece to the armholes, which keeps the construction relatively straightforward. The main body uses treble crochet while the borders are worked in double crochet, creating a clean traditional finish. The designer also notes that it can be made in one color, contrasting colors, or stripes.

<CloudImage src="https://res.cloudinary.com/dbjxr1kdv/image/upload/v1787148517/DSCN0201_-_Copy_eyvtcx.webp" alt="## Classic Crochet Baby Cardigan" />

### Pattern Details

* **Skill Level:** Easy
* **Designer:** Marianna Mel
* **Original Source:** [Marianna's Lazy Daisy Days / Ravelry](https://mariannaslazydaisydays.blogspot.com/2014/01/crochet-baby-cardigan-jacket.html)
* **Size:** 9–12 months
* **Yarn:** DK weight

For a classic nursery wardrobe, cream, oatmeal, pale blue, dusty pink, or soft gray would all be beautiful choices. A contrasting border gives the cardigan a particularly traditional appearance, while a single neutral shade makes it easy to pair with almost any baby outfit.

**Pattern:** [Visit Marianna's Lazy Daisy Days for the free pattern](https://mariannaslazydaisydays.blogspot.com/2014/01/crochet-baby-cardigan-jacket.html)

# Final Thoughts

Classic baby cardigans are some of the most rewarding crochet garments to make because they combine practicality with the sentimental feeling of something handmade. A tiny newborn cardigan can become a coming-home outfit, while a larger 6–12-month design can be worn throughout a baby's first year and saved afterward as a keepsake.

For a timeless look, **cream, ivory, oatmeal, dusty blue, blush, sage, and soft gray** are excellent yarn choices. Simple wooden buttons, pearl-style buttons, contrasting borders, shell stitches, bobbles, and delicate ruffles can also give a basic cardigan a beautiful heirloom character.

If you're making one as a baby-shower gift, consider pairing the cardigan with **matching crochet booties, a baby bonnet, or a small blanket**. A coordinated handmade set feels especially thoughtful and gives the new family something they can treasure long after the baby has outgrown it.

**Always visit the original designer's page before starting to confirm the current pattern, sizing, yarn requirements, and whether the free version is still available.**
