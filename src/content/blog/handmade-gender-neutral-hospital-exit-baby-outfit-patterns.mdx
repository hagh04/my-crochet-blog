# How to Style a Handmade Gender-Neutral Hospital Exit Outfit

The easiest way to create a coordinated handmade outfit is to start with one main piece, such as a knitted romper or simple cotton bodysuit, and then add two or three small accessories. A bonnet or hat, tiny booties, and a lightweight cardigan are more than enough to create a polished look without making the outfit bulky.

For colors, consider **cream, oatmeal, beige, sage green, soft gray, muted yellow, dusty blue, or natural brown**. These shades photograph beautifully and can be used regardless of whether the baby's sex is known before birth. Soft cotton and baby-friendly yarns are particularly useful for pieces worn close to newborn skin.

For colder weather, layer a handmade cardigan over the main outfit and add mittens and booties. For warmer weather, keep the outfit lightweight with a breathable cotton romper, bonnet, and booties.

## Final Thoughts

A handmade gender-neutral hospital exit baby outfit is more than just clothing—it is a little piece of love created specifically for one of life's biggest moments. Whether you knit a complete romper or crochet only a tiny bonnet and pair of booties, handmade details can make those first photographs and memories even more meaningful.

Choose soft yarn, comfortable construction, practical closures, and gentle neutral colors. Most importantly, check the finished size and fit carefully before using any handmade newborn garment, and follow the pattern designer's instructions for yarn and construction.
