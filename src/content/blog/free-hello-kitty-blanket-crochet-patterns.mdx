Instead of crocheting one enormous character image, you create individual squares featuring Hello Kitty's sweet face, pink bow, ears, eyes, nose, and whiskers. The sample uses five colors and a 3 mm hook, with each completed square measuring approximately 2.95 × 2.95 inches. Because the motif is made separately, you can create as many squares as needed for a baby blanket, lapghan, throw, or larger afghan.

## Final Thoughts

These **free Hello Kitty blanket crochet patterns** give you several different ways to create a handmade character blanket without purchasing a pattern.

If you want a **large traditional graphghan**, Kendra's 120 × 100 graph is the most ambitious choice. For a **modular blanket**, the Hello Kitty granny squares are much easier to customize. The **Kitty Hexagon Blanket** is perfect if you want something more unusual, while the newer **Hello Kitty Blanket Square** offers a straightforward way to build a blanket gradually.

The biggest advantage of using individual motifs is flexibility—you can decide the blanket's final size, color palette, and layout as you crochet. That makes these patterns especially useful for handmade birthday gifts, children's blankets, nursery décor, and cozy throws for Hello Kitty fans.
