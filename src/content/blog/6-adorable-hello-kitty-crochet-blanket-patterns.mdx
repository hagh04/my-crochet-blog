If you love Hello Kitty and crochet, a handmade blanket is one of the sweetest ways to combine both. From colorful C2C graphgans to charming baby blankets and smaller lovey-style projects, there are several ways to bring Hello Kitty’s recognizable bow, whiskers, and cheerful expression into a crochet project.

Whether you want to make a cozy blanket for a child, a nursery accent, or a colorful throw for a Sanrio-loving fan, these **Hello Kitty crochet blanket patterns** offer plenty of inspiration. I’ve focused on free patterns and designs that are different from the Hello Kitty crochet patterns we’ve already featured.

## 1. Hello Kitty Free C2C Crochet Blanket Pattern With Graph

The **Hello Kitty Free C2C Crochet Blanket Pattern With Graph by Magic Yarn Pixels** is a colorful character blanket featuring Hello Kitty’s face against a bright pink background decorated with flowers. The pattern includes both a pixel graph and written row-by-row instructions, making it useful for crocheters who like having both visual and written guidance. The graph measures **80 squares wide by 100 squares high**, creating a substantial rectangular character design.

* **Skill Level:** Intermediate
* **Designer:** Ariana Wimsett / Magic Yarn Pixels
* **Project Type:** C2C crochet blanket
* **Technique:** Corner-to-corner
* **Graph Size:** 80 × 100 squares
* **Colors:** Pink, white, black, yellow
* **Written Pattern:** Yes
* **Pattern:** Free

The bright pink background makes this design especially fun for a girl's bedroom or colorful crochet collection. You can also change the background color while keeping Hello Kitty's traditional white face, black features, yellow nose, and pink bow. If you're comfortable with C2C color changes, this would make a fantastic statement blanket.

## 2. Doudou Hello Kitty

The **Doudou Hello Kitty by Les Récréations de Mayleen** is a particularly sweet option if you're looking for a smaller comfort blanket rather than a large afghan. Ravelry categorizes it under blankets and describes the design as a three-dimensional, modular project with colorwork. The free pattern was published in 2018 and uses **Aran-weight yarn with a 4 mm hook**. It is available in both English and French.

* **Designer:** Les Récréations de Mayleen
* **Project Type:** Doudou / baby comfort blanket
* **Technique:** Modular, colorwork
* **Yarn:** Aran weight
* **Hook:** 4 mm

This design would make a lovely handmade baby gift because its smaller format is easier for little hands to hold. Traditional white, pink, black, and yellow create the recognizable Hello Kitty look, while softer pastel shades could give the project a more modern nursery aesthetic.

## 4. Hello Kitty Pixel Party

The **Hello Kitty Pixel Party by Jennifer Santos** is a fun C2C option for crocheters who enjoy building larger projects from individual graph squares. The free Ravelry pattern creates a **36 × 36 pixel Hello Kitty square**, measuring approximately **46 × 46 cm** with the recommended gauge. It was originally part of the SuperCute Pixel Party crochet-along, where individual character squares could be combined into a larger blanket.

<CloudImage src="https://res.cloudinary.com/dbjxr1kdv/image/upload/v1791107914/202509295920457755952065356_smal_zx9e58.webp" alt="## Hello Kitty Pixel Party" />

### Pattern Details

* **Skill Level:** Not specified
* **Designer:** Jennifer Santos
* **Project Type:** C2C pixel square
* **Technique:** Corner-to-corner
* **Chart Size:** 36 × 36 squares
* **Finished Size:** Approximately 46 × 46 cm
* **Hook:** 3.5 mm
* **Pattern:** Free Ravelry download

One of the best things about this pattern is its flexibility. You can crochet the Hello Kitty square on its own as a wall hanging, pillow front, or small blanket, or combine it with additional pixel squares to build a larger character-themed afghan. It is also a great way to experiment with C2C before tackling a much larger graph.

**Pattern:** [Visit Ravelry for the free Hello Kitty Pixel Party pattern](https://www.ravelry.com/patterns/library/hello-kitty-pixel-party?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

### Final Thoughts

These **Hello Kitty crochet blanket patterns** give you several different ways to bring the beloved character into a handmade project. If you want a large statement piece, the **Magic Yarn Pixels C2C blanket** is the strongest choice. For something smaller and baby-friendly, the **Doudou Hello Kitty** or **Hello Kitty Mini Blanket** is worth considering. And if you enjoy pixel crochet, the **Hello Kitty Pixel Party** square gives you plenty of flexibility for creating a larger custom blanket.

Before starting any project, check the original designer's page for the latest pattern availability and usage terms, as free-pattern pages can change over time.
