Granny squares are one of the most versatile crochet motifs you can make. A simple square can be transformed into a cozy blanket, colorful bag, decorative pillow, cardigan, baby project, or beautiful piece of home décor. While the traditional granny square is a wonderful beginner project, there are countless creative variations to explore, including solid squares, sunbursts, hearts, flowers, spirals, lattice designs, and textured stitches. We’ve gathered **14 free granny square crochet patterns** featured in Sarah Maker’s original collection so you can discover new designs and find inspiration for your next project.

## Final Thoughts

The beauty of **granny square crochet patterns** is that you can start with one basic idea and take it in countless directions. Classic and solid squares are perfect for beginners, while floral, spiral, textured, and graphic motifs give experienced crocheters plenty of new techniques to explore.

These patterns are also fantastic for **using up your yarn stash**. Small amounts of leftover yarn can become flowers, hearts, colorful centers, or individual rounds, eventually coming together to create a much larger project.

Whether you're planning a cozy blanket, baby gift, decorative pillow, crochet bag, or colorful home décor project, these 14 designs give you plenty of inspiration.
