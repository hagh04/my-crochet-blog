A crochet pencil case is a small project that can be both useful and fun to make. Whether you need something for school supplies, crochet hooks, colored pencils, or everyday stationery, a handmade pouch lets you choose your favorite colors, textures, and closures. Many of these projects also use relatively small amounts of yarn, making them excellent stash-busting projects. From a cheerful candy-striped pouch to an adorable fox and a tapestry heart design, these **10 free crochet pencil case patterns** offer plenty of inspiration for your next quick crochet project.

## Final Thoughts

These **10 free crochet pencil case patterns** offer plenty of options, whether you're looking for something simple, colorful, cute, or completely customizable. Beginners can start with the **Back-to-School Pencil Case**, **Candy Stripe Pencil Case**, or **Basic Crochet Pencil Case**, while more experienced crocheters may enjoy the **Tapestry Heart** or **C2C Write Stuff** designs.

For children, the **Crayon Pencil Case** and **Fox Pencil Case** are especially fun choices. If you want something practical for everyday use, the **Annie Design Crochet** zippered case and **Basic Crochet Pencil Case** offer compact designs that can easily fit into a school bag or work tote.

Don't be afraid to experiment with your yarn stash. Pencil cases are small projects, so they're perfect for trying new colors, using leftover yarn, or adding personalized details such as initials, appliqués, stripes, or tapestry motifs.
