There are endless ways to reinvent the granny square, and these 10 patterns prove that you don't have to keep making the same traditional designs. This collection focuses on **new granny square crochet patterns** that weren't included in our previous selections. You'll find modern geometric constructions, dimensional flowers, textured centers, unusual color arrangements, and decorative motifs that can turn a simple collection of squares into something truly special. Whether you're planning a blanket, bag, pillow, cardigan, or another crochet project, these fresh designs are worth adding to your pattern list.

---

## 1. Belita Square

The **Belita Square** from Underground Crafter is an interesting eight-inch granny square constructed from four smaller squares. Rather than creating one motif from the center outward, the design combines smaller elements into a larger finished square, giving it a distinctive geometric appearance. It is part of the designer's 2026 Granny Square Month Crochet Along and is available as a free pattern.

<CloudImage src="https://res.cloudinary.com/dcgvm3vqc/image/upload/v1789333168/Belita-Square-free-crochet-pattern-by-Underground-Crafter-FB-600x314_retukv.jpg" alt="Belita 8 Crochet Square Pattern" />

### Pattern Details

* **Skill Level:** Intermediate
* **Designer:** Marie Segares — Underground Crafter
* **Finished Size:** Approximately 8 × 8 inches
* **Technique:** Four-square construction
* **Best Feature:** Geometric composition
* **Tutorial:** Free written pattern
* **Style:** Modern and colorful

The three-color sample creates a beautiful layered effect, but this square would also look striking in a two-color palette. Use contrasting colors for the smaller squares if you want each section to stand out, or keep the colors coordinated for a more seamless blanket.

**Free Pattern & Sample Image:** [Underground Crafter — Belita Square](https://undergroundcrafter.com/2026/06/01/belita-8-crochet-square-pattern/)

## 4. Midnight Bloom Granny Square

The **Midnight Bloom Granny Square** from Knotions has a dramatic, sophisticated appearance. It is worked from the center outward using six shades of fingering-weight yarn, creating a gradual color transition from a light center to a deep outer edge. An open lacy border completes the design.

<CloudImage src="https://res.cloudinary.com/dcgvm3vqc/image/upload/v1789333410/midnight-bloom-crochet-granny-square_q5y7ji.jpg" alt="Midnight Bloom Crochet Granny Square" />

### Pattern Details

* **Skill Level:** Intermediate
* **Designer:** Knotions
* **Yarn:** Fingering-weight yarn
* **Colors:** Six-shade gradient
* **Technique:** Double crochet and long front-post treble stitches
* **Best Feature:** Gradient starburst effect
* **Tutorial:** Free written pattern
* **Style:** Elegant and dramatic

The original blue gradient gives this square its midnight-inspired appearance, but the same concept would work beautifully in sunset colors such as coral, orange, pink, and deep purple. It would make a particularly striking centerpiece for a sophisticated blanket.

**Free Pattern & Sample Image:** [Knotions — Midnight Bloom Granny Square](https://knotions.com/free-crochet-granny-square-pattern-may-2026/)

## Final Thoughts

These **10 granny square patterns are all new additions** compared with the previous lists. They offer some especially interesting construction ideas, including the four-square **Belita**, the on-point **Delia**, the floral **Gabriela**, the gradient **Midnight Bloom**, and the dimensional **Lilah**.

For a quick project, the **Star Granny Square** and **Heart Granny Square** are excellent choices. If you want something with more texture, try the **Midnight Bloom**, **Lilah Afghan Square**, or **Flower Granny Square**.

You can also mix these motifs together in a sampler blanket. Keeping the final round in the same color is an easy way to make very different granny squares look like they belong together.
