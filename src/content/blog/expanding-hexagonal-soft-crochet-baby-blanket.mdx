If you're looking for a baby blanket that's both eye-catching and incredibly cozy, the **Expanding Hexagonal Soft Baby Blanket** is a wonderful project to add to your crochet list. Unlike traditional square blankets, this clever hexagonal design grows evenly from the center outward, allowing you to easily customize the final size. The soft texture, modern shape, and seamless construction make it a beautiful keepsake that parents will treasure for years. Whether you're making a stroller blanket or a larger nursery throw, this versatile pattern is a joy to crochet.
