Granny square blankets have come a long way from the traditional designs many of us remember. Today's crochet designers are combining classic granny-square techniques with modern colors, unusual motifs, geometric arrangements, and creative construction methods to create blankets that feel fresh and stylish. We've gathered **10 free granny square blanket patterns** covering a variety of styles and skill levels. Whether you're looking for a colorful scrap blanket, a modern baby blanket, or a timeless throw for your home, there's something here to inspire your next project.

The **Grandmother's Flower Garden Crochet Blanket** from Make & Do Crew takes inspiration from vintage quilt designs and transforms that classic look into a beautiful crochet project. The hexagons can be joined as you go, making the project portable and allowing you to customize the finished size. The pattern also works wonderfully with yarn cakes or leftover yarn.

The sample combines colorful yarn-cake shades with a clean white border around each motif. ## Final Thoughts

These **10 granny square blanket patterns** show just how versatile this classic crochet technique can be. From the vintage-inspired **Grandmother's Flower Garden Blanket** and floral **Southern Blooms Blanket** to the bold geometric **Mabel Blanket** and colorful **Random Granny Afghan**, there's a style for almost every crocheter.

If you're a beginner, the **Sweet Granny Baby Blanket**, **Modern Granny Blanket**, or **Granny Stripe Blanket** are great places to start. If you're looking for a more intricate project, try the **Mabel Blanket**, **Love Triangles Blanket**, or **Circle of Hope Blanket**.

The best part is that you can personalize almost every design with your own yarn colors. A simple change of palette can completely transform the finished blanket.
